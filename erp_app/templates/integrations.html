modified_sinceRows changed at or after this UTC time.
after_idWith modified_since: continue after this row, i.e. (modified_at, id) > (modified_since, after_id). Rows that share one timestamp are never skipped or repeated.
limitRows per page, default 500, max 1000.
PagingRows come oldest change first, then by id. Each page has next (modified_since + after_id) and next_url. A page shorter than limit means you've caught up.
Errors401 with no or a wrong key, 400 for a bad time or after_id without modified_since.

5. Live simulator

Last error{{ stats.last_error }}

While it runs, each tick it creates a new order for a real customer with real products at list price (order date and modifieddate = now, comment = 'SIMULATED'). It often moves open simulated orders on (In process, Approved, Shipped, now and then Cancelled), and shipping takes the stock out of Finished Goods Storage. Now and then a customer moves house: a new address row, with their home address pointing to it. Each change fires its webhook.

It never edits Microsoft's historical orders. Everything it changes is in erp_app.simulator_log.

Check it through the API: curl.exe -H "X-API-Key: <key>" "{{ base }}/api/v1/sales-orders?modified_since=<5 minutes ago, UTC>"
